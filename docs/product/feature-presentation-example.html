The goal

More EU orders, and nothing else gets worse

Outcome metric
2.1% → 2.6%

EU checkout conversion, measured 4 weeks after release, against the 4 weeks before.

Guardrails: must stay the same

  • Refund rate stays under 1.5%
  • Product page still loads in under 300 ms
  • No change at all for US shoppers

In scope

  • EUR on product, cart, checkout
  • Daily exchange rate
  • EUR on the receipt

Not in scope

  • Charging cards in EUR
  • VAT or tax changes
  • Other currencies
